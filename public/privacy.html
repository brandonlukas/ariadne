ariadne. privacy

Privacy

Short, because there is almost nothing to disclose.

no server, no accounts, no tracking

Ariadne is a static page. There is no backend, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies set by ariadne. Your seed papers, woven corpora, stars and hides, and any AI-generated text are stored only in your browser's localStorage, on your device. Clearing your seeds, using "turn off — forget key," or clearing site data removes them. We never see any of it — there is no "we" to send it to.

what your browser talks to

OpenAlex — every weave queries the OpenAlex API directly from your browser for paper metadata. Those requests include a maintainer contact email (per OpenAlex's polite-use policy) — not your identity. OpenAlex sees the paper IDs and searches you request, from your IP address, under their terms.

Publisher image hosts — the gallery view loads figure images directly from arXiv/ar5iv, Springer Nature, PLOS, bioRxiv/medRxiv, and eLife. Like any embedded image on the web, those hosts see your IP request the file.

OpenRouter (optional) — only if you paste an API key. The key is stored in your browser's localStorage and sent only to OpenRouter to authenticate your own requests. When AI is on, the titles and abstracts of your seed papers (and of papers you open) are sent to OpenRouter for processing under their privacy policy. Turning AI off deletes the key and everything it generated. Never paste your key into a device you don't trust.

GitHub Pages — hosts this site and may log standard access data (IP, user agent) per GitHub's privacy statement.

sharing links

The "copy link" URL contains only the OpenAlex IDs of your seed papers. Anyone with the link can see which seeds it encodes and rebuild the same weave; your stars, hides, and AI text are not in the link and stay on your device.
